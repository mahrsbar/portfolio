Redesigning the Remnant Labs website

Remnant Labs is a fantasy-anime universe spanning several connected games. As lead front-end developer I ran the overhaul of the studio's website, from audience research and brand voice to prototypes and the Vue front end that shipped.

  • Web traffic up 125%After launch
  • Lead front endResearch, brand voice, design and the Vue build
  • 3 interactive character cardsPrototyped on CodePen
Role
Lead front-end developer; research, design, and build
Stack
Figma, Vue, Firebase, GSAP

Research

I started with the genre: who plays fantasy-anime strategy games, what competing studios sound like, and where a new brand could stand out. With the team we set a brand foundation:

  • Brand voice. Graphics heavily influenced by Japanese anime, a whimsical tone, a bright and adventurous atmosphere.
  • Audience. Casual and strategy players, teens and young adults in North America, Japan, China, and Korea.
  • Tone. A casual role-playing voice across the site and in-game text, used whenever developers and game masters speak to players.

Prototyping

Low-fidelity wireframes first, so the team and stakeholders could test layouts quickly before any visual design.

Homepage

A transparent navigation bar with deliberately few options (Hick's law) for fast decisions, a trailer video showing the game's art and concept, and a clear call to action beside the brand description.

Games page

I proposed and designed a games page showcasing the whole Remnant ecosystem so players could browse between titles.

Implementation

Character card components

Character cards are central to the game, so I built three interactive versions exploring how to present avatars and descriptions.

Results

Web traffic went up 125% after launch, and engagement and conversion rose with it. Simpler navigation, the new games page and art matched to the game made the site feel like part of the game.
